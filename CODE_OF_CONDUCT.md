# Community Conduct

WHOAYO's community should make careful, respectful collaboration possible.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- respect privacy, consent and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct and doxxing;
- never pressure someone to reveal private concepts, credentials or identity;
- represent evidence and uncertainty honestly;
- accept good-faith moderation intended to protect people and the project.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens safety, privacy or productive collaboration. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid republishing harmful material.

After publication, report conduct concerns through the private maintainer contact listed on the repository profile. Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue. A dedicated conduct contact is still an owner launch gate.
