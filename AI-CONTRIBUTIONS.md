# AI-Assisted Contributions

AI assistance is allowed when it is disclosed and reviewed. It does not become an author, copyright owner, approver or accountable maintainer.

For a pull request, the human submitter must:

1. identify material AI assistance in the template;
2. understand the submitted behavior and dependency changes;
3. verify generated code with relevant tests and threat-boundary review;
4. confirm no private source, credential or restricted content entered an unapproved model;
5. preserve third-party attribution and license obligations;
6. avoid claims that a model independently proved correctness or security.

Generated output is treated as untrusted input. Maintainers may request prompt/context provenance where it can be shared safely, but nobody should disclose a private concept or provider secret to satisfy review.
